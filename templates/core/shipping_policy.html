Shipping Policy.

Delivery times, costs and practical information.

Cost and delivery time

The final shipping cost appears in your order summary before confirmation. Our team will confirm timing according to your governorate and product availability.

Inspecting your order

Please inspect the packaging on delivery and report visible damage or missing items immediately. Keep the packaging and take clear photographs.

Failed delivery

Delivery may be rescheduled if we cannot reach you or the address is incorrect. A new delivery attempt may incur an additional charge.
