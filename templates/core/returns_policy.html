Returns & Exchanges.

Clear steps if something is not right.

Requesting a return or exchange

Contact us with your order number, the product condition and photographs of the issue within the applicable legal period. The product must remain in its original condition with all accessories unless it is defective.

Defective products

We inspect the product and, when a manufacturing defect is confirmed, provide repair, replacement or refund according to applicable law and warranty terms.

Exceptions

Products that have been installed, modified or misused may not be accepted unless the request concerns a confirmed manufacturing defect.
